**Phase 0 · Product-architecture review · 2026-11-13.** This map follows the separately supplied `CODEX_ENGINEERING_SPEC_V1.md` and the product decisions below. It uses repository baseline `d712f12` and a read-only inspection of Figma pages 02–04, including frame renders, node metadata and page 04’s click destinations. Page 01 is an early concept. ## Page 02 · Refined desktop states

The five 1440 × 900 frames reuse a centered portrait in a warm interior, top and bottom scrims, a top-right self-view, a quiet connection indicator, a tutor name and a bottom dock labeled Mic, Cam, CC, Aide and Fin. The tutor layer is named “Tutor video / image placeholder (replace with live stream)” and renders a still. The self-view is a 210 × 144 component instance. Maintain the visual continuity as overlays change. The current app places lessons in a card beside a sidebar and a permanent tools/camera panel. The tutor surface, call controls and anchored overlay regions are new. [Current app](../src/components/learning-app.tsx) · [Current styles](../src/app/globals.css)

Preserve the immersive, video-first composition with a stable tutor stage and instructional overlays. It must work with learner camera permission off, denied or revoked. Tutor rendering and learner self-view are separate. The stage should accept different presence renderers while avatar technology remains open for evaluation. A still or scripted asset in a future preview needs a truthful label.

Page 03’s [six branches](https://www.figma.com/design/GV3y4FmpF1C8CIqFMcurfa?node-id=10-87) need explicit outcomes. Help opens a scoped aid and preserves focus. Silence allows time, then an optional check-in and mic check with zero failure evidence. Unrecognized speech offers correction, replay, headset check or typing. Camera denied/off leaves voice and keyboard intact. Disconnect stops the media, re-reads the last committed checkpoint and shows retry/text/resume. Early success changes an aid only when the applicable policy threshold is met, with manual restoration and accessibility preferences taking precedence. Test each branch with the deterministic driver, then repeat real media branches on the laptop.

The spec’s `idle`, `connecting`, `processing`, `recovering`, `offline` and `ended` have no standalone page 04 frames. Keep the same surface with a relevant status or overlay. ## Reuse and decisions for review

- Reuse the App Router, French document language, error boundary, manifest/base-path pattern, safe public cache allowlist, browser media teardown patterns and stable curriculum IDs. Keep the existing guided app and tests as the Pages compatibility path until a separately approved migration. The immersive shell needs new styles and controls. Split device lifecycle from rendering before sharing `CameraPreview`. Preserve current storage validators for migration. Keep the existing speech-pattern evaluator scoped to legacy practice. [Media](../src/components/media-tools.tsx) · [Curriculum](../src/domain/curriculum.ts) · [Learner state](../src/domain/learner.ts) · [PWA worker](../public/sw.js)
- Show English and French instructional overlays from the beginning, including the initial model and invitation. Refined frames `6:72` and `6:114` show an English phrase with French task text or status. Design review determines the French meaning aid’s placement, maximum lines and relationship to CC versus Aide. Fade individual aids only after demonstrated independence for the relevant capability. Help restores an aid immediately, and learner accessibility preferences take precedence.
- Confirm pause/resume placement, recovery and exit copy, off-camera self-view (hide or compact tile), optional keyboard positioning, and whether “En ligne” should be replaced by a state-specific French label. These production variants have no matching approved refined frames.
- Confirm portrait asset rights and tutor naming, accepted focus crop at 1280 × 720, and visual labeling of sample audio or a still portrait. The repository’s `assets/` and `public/assets/` contain app icons. The source portrait, speech clips and any instructional imagery must be approved and supplied or exported in Phase 1. Use Figma frame renders as references. For repair, approve the diagnostic content, visual assets and no-camera equivalent. Pointing in the Figma copy is illustrative and requires a separately consented, purposeful implementation.
- Approve the accessible meaning check and transfer variant. Decide whether the learner can opt into a brief listening-only probe while keeping immediate access to both languages. Aided learning remains useful, including when the learner needs captions on every turn. [Implementation plan](IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md)
